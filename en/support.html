FAQ

Can I use Premium Unlimited on another device?
Yes. On a device signed in with the same Apple ID, tap “Restore Purchases” on the Premium tab.

Can I exchange money at the rates shown?
The App shows ECB reference rates. They differ from the rates used by banks and currency exchanges (which include fees and spreads).

Does the App show ads?
No. Neither the free version nor Premium shows ads.
